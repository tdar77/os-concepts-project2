Explanation of implementation:

After carefully analyzing the project requirements, a lot of decisions were made that shaped
this implementation.

First, to ensure accurate FIFO for students walking into the waiting room, a circular ring
buffer (similar to the one I used in the barber project) was used, to ensure fairness. However,
this buffer only determined who came first, the coordinator had to determine who the tutors 
helped first. This was accomplished by essentially creating an array of linked lists, where
each element in the array corresponded to a priority level (which depends on the required number
of visits).

One issue I ran into was determining how the tutor would realize that their are students waiting
for help. The decision was made for the coordinator to post the semaphore, but only after the student
was addded to the queue. Since the addition was gated by a mutex, we ensured correctness. The coordinator
also posts 1 final semaphore to each tutor when the run is done to indicate that no students need help 
anymore.

A bug I ran into was not handling thread creation failure, which only occurred during high-stress
runs. The solution was fairly simple, but it was causing forever spins in certain scenarios.
